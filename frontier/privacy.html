Frontier by Delfos Hub extension

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Frontier by Delfos Hub is a non-custodial Terra Classic wallet. Your keys stay in your browser. Delfos Hub does not run accounts, does not collect personal data and cannot see or move your funds.

In short: no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, nothing sold. The extension talks to public Terra Classic services to show balances and send transactions you approve, and nothing else.

1. What is stored, and where

  • Recovery phrases are encrypted with your 8-digit PIN (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds, AES-GCM-256) and kept only in the extension's local storage in your browser. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The unlocked phrase lives only in the browser's session memory while Frontier is unlocked, and is cleared when it locks or the browser closes.
  • Settings kept locally: wallet names and addresses, watched addresses, the list of sites you connected, your language, the auto-lock time, a short list of transfers sent from this browser, and cached public market data.

Removing a wallet or the extension deletes this data from your browser. Delfos Hub has no copy and cannot recover a wallet for you; only your recovery phrase can.

2. Network requests

To work, the extension sends requests to the services below. They receive your IP address, as any website does, and the public wallet address being looked up. They never receive your recovery phrase or PIN.

  • Terra Classic public nodes (terra-classic-lcd.publicnode.com, lcd.terra-classic.hexxagon.io): balances, staking, governance, history, simulating and broadcasting transactions you approve.
  • CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com): LUNC and USTC prices.
  • Delfos Hub market proxy (orbitwire-proxy.delfoshub-team.workers.dev): a public list of trading pools used for swap routes and token prices. No address is sent to it.
  • Delfos P2P widget (p2p.delfos-hub.net): loaded only when you open the P2P market. It sees your address and never holds a key; every trade is approved by you with your PIN.
  • Token images from the addresses token issuers publish (for example IPFS gateways).

3. Websites you connect

A website can see your address only after you approve the connection. Every message or transaction it asks you to sign opens a Frontier window showing what it does; nothing is signed without your approval. You can disconnect any site in Settings > Connected sites.

4. Permissions

  • storage - to keep the encrypted wallets and settings described above.
  • alarms - to lock the wallet after the idle time you choose.
  • Access to websites - to offer the Frontier connection to Terra Classic websites (the same way Keplr does). The extension reads nothing from the pages you visit; it only answers requests a page makes to it.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, cookies or advertising identifiers in the extension.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose unrelated to the wallet, and not to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

6. Children

Frontier is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
